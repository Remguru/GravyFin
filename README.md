GravyFin

GravyFin is a deliberately barebones Jellyfin client for Apple TV.
GravyFin Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

GravyFin is a Jellyfin client for Apple TV designed to provide a simple way to browse and play media from a Jellyfin server selected by the user.
Information We Collect

GravyFin does not collect, sell, share, or use personal information for advertising, analytics, tracking, or marketing purposes.

GravyFin does not include advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking services.
Jellyfin Account Information

To connect to a Jellyfin server, GravyFin uses the server address and login credentials provided by the user.

Your login information is used only to communicate with the Jellyfin server you choose. GravyFin may securely store your server address, username, user ID, and Jellyfin access token on your Apple TV using Apple's Keychain services so that you can remain signed in. Your Jellyfin password is not stored after authentication.
Media and Playback Information

GravyFin communicates directly with your Jellyfin server to retrieve media libraries, artwork, playback information, watch progress, and other information required for the app to function.

This information is not collected by the developer of GravyFin.
Local Network Access

GravyFin may access your local network to discover and connect to Jellyfin servers. Information discovered on your local network is used only to provide this functionality and is not transmitted to the developer.
Third Parties

GravyFin does not sell or share user information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

Your Jellyfin server and its administrator may have their own privacy and data-handling practices. GravyFin does not control the operation of your Jellyfin server.
Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if GravyFin's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will include a revised effective date.
